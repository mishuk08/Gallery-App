# 📸 Gallery

### Your Photos, Your Privacy.

A simple, modern, and user-friendly photo gallery application designed to help you browse, organize, and manage your photos and videos. It also includes a Hide Photos feature to keep your private memories away from the main gallery.

## ✨ Features

- 🖼️ Browse photos and videos.
- 📁 Organize photos into albums.
- 🔒 Hide private photos from the main gallery.
- 🔐 Privacy-focused photo management.
- 🎨 Clean and user-friendly interface.
- ⚡ Simple and smooth gallery experience.

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

Your personal memories deserve privacy. Gallery allows you to hide selected photos so they don't appear in the main gallery.

## ⚠️ Important: Backup Your Photos

**Hiding photos does not mean they are permanently safe.**

Always keep a separate backup of your important photos, including photos hidden using this application.

- Keep backup copies in secure cloud storage or an external drive.
- Do not rely on this app as your only storage.
- Clearing app data or uninstalling the application may result in losing hidden photos, depending on how the app stores them.
- Verify your backup before deleting any original photos.

> **Hide your photos for privacy, but back them up for safety.**
